# Product Owner (PO)

## Overview

The Product Owner is responsible for defining the product vision, managing backlog priorities, and ensuring alignment between the development team and business objectives. In the context of AI/Quality Sentinel, the PO acts as the bridge between stakeholders who want better code quality and the team implementing the solution.

---

## How to Help the Project

### 1. **Define Quality Standards**
- Establish what "quality" means for the project
- Define success metrics for code analysis and detection accuracy
- Create acceptance criteria that reflect business expectations

### 2. **Manage the Feature Backlog**
- Prioritize which quality checks should be implemented first
- Balance between AI analysis features and user experience improvements
- Make decisions on integrations with Jira, GitHub, or GitLab

### 3. **Clarify Business Requirements**
- Explain the business impact of identifying misaligned code
- Help developers understand how this tool reduces rework and costs
- Define scope and limitations of the AI Sentinel capabilities

### 4. **Validate Solutions**
- Review completed features against acceptance criteria
- Ensure the tool actually solves the original business problem
- Gather feedback from early users and iterate

### 5. **Stakeholder Communication**
- Keep stakeholders informed about progress
- Present business value and ROI of the project
- Manage expectations about AI capabilities and limitations

---

## Key Responsibilities

- ✅ Create and refine user stories with clear acceptance criteria
- ✅ Maintain and prioritize the product backlog
- ✅ Participate in sprint planning and refinement sessions
- ✅ Validate completed work before release
- ✅ Communicate project status to stakeholders
- ✅ Make product decisions and trade-offs

---

## Required Skills

**Technical:**
- Basic understanding of software development concepts
- Familiarity with AI/ML capabilities and limitations
- Knowledge of development tools (Jira, GitHub, GitLab)

**Soft Skills:**
- Strong communication and storytelling
- Prioritization and decision-making
- Stakeholder management
- Ability to translate business needs to technical requirements

---

## Experience Level

**Minimum:** Mid-level product manager with some tech experience
**Ideal:** 3-5 years in product management, preferably in software development or quality domains
