# Scrum Master (SM)

## Overview

The Scrum Master facilitates agile ceremonies, removes blockers, and ensures the team follows agile practices. In AI/Quality Sentinel, the SM ensures smooth collaboration between Product Owner, developers, QA, and other team members while maintaining team velocity and focus on project goals.

---

## How to Help the Project

### 1. **Facilitate Agile Ceremonies**
- Organize and run sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Ensure ceremonies stay focused and time-boxed
- Use these meetings to identify and track blockers and improvements

### 2. **Remove Impediments**
- Identify obstacles preventing the team from making progress
- Work to resolve blockers related to infrastructure, dependencies, or coordination
- Escalate issues that need management attention

### 3. **Foster Team Collaboration**
- Break down silos between backend, frontend, QA, and other roles
- Ensure knowledge sharing and pair programming opportunities
- Help the team self-organize and make decisions

### 4. **Track Metrics and Improve**
- Monitor velocity, burndown, and cycle time
- Analyze sprint retrospectives for continuous improvement
- Implement process improvements based on team feedback

### 5. **Ensure Quality Culture**
- Protect the team from mid-sprint changes and distractions
- Coach the team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Help the team understand how quality work reduces future rework

---

## Key Responsibilities

- ✅ Organize and facilitate all agile ceremonies
- ✅ Maintain the sprint board and backlog health
- ✅ Identify and help resolve team impediments
- ✅ Coach team members on agile practices
- ✅ Collect and analyze sprint metrics
- ✅ Facilitate retrospectives and drive improvements

---

## Required Skills

**Technical:**
- Deep knowledge of Scrum and Kanban methodologies
- Understanding of software development lifecycle
- Familiarity with agile tools (Jira, Azure DevOps, etc.)

**Soft Skills:**
- Excellent facilitation and listening skills
- Servant leadership mindset
- Problem-solving and conflict resolution
- Adaptability and continuous learning
- Patience and empathy

---

## Experience Level

**Minimum:** Certified Scrum Master (CSM) with at least 1-2 years of experience
**Ideal:** 3-5 years as Scrum Master, preferably in software development or tech companies
